      <p class="lead">Build every idea behind modern image models in pure Python. No frameworks, no GPU - just the actual math, running in your browser.</p>
      <p>Start with the convolution, the operation that lets networks see. Compress images with autoencoders, meet the U-Net that powers every serious image model, pit a generator against a critic in a GAN, and finish by building diffusion - the technology inside Stable Diffusion and DALL-E - from first principles.</p>
      <p>Prerequisite: comfort with <a href="/courses/machine-learning/neural-networks-from-scratch/">Neural Networks from Scratch</a> in the sister course, <a href="/courses/machine-learning/">Machine Learning: From Zero to LLMs</a>.</p>
