# Learn Ruby with an AI Teacher

Books and pre-recorded courses are one-way: they talk, you listen. An AI assistant flips that - it asks what you know, adapts when you struggle, and is awake at 2 AM when you finally have time to study. Combined with our structured tracks, it becomes the closest thing to a personal tutor that has ever existed for self-learners.

## How programming education evolved

- **Books:** deep but static - no feedback, no adaptation.
- **Video courses:** structured, but still one-way and fixed-pace.
- **AI teachers:** interactive, personalised, and available around the clock.

## What your AI teacher can do

- **Schedule daily classes.** Ask it to message you a lesson every morning and hold you accountable.
- **Build a custom curriculum.** Point it at our [Ruby Basics](/courses/ruby-basics/) chapter list and have it expand each chapter into explanations matched to your level.
- **Set homework.** After every lesson, ask for three exercises - one easy, one medium, one that scares you slightly.
- **Review your code instantly.** Paste your solution and get line-by-line feedback, style suggestions, and the "why" behind every correction.
- **Answer without judgement.** Ask the same question ten times. It will answer ten times, differently each time if you need it.

## Set it up in five minutes

1. Open your AI assistant of choice (ChatGPT, Claude, Kimi, or any other).
2. Start a dedicated conversation and paste this briefing:

   > You are my personal Ruby teacher. I am working through the Ruby Basics course at rubylearning.in/courses/ruby-basics/. Teach me one chapter at a time: explain the concept with two examples, quiz me, then give me homework. Adjust your pace based on my answers. Start by asking what I already know.

3. Fix a daily slot - even 20 minutes - and let the streak carry you.
4. When a chapter references our runnable examples, run them here in the browser and paste your experiments back into the chat.

## Why this works better than either alone

- **Accountability:** daily check-ins beat motivation.
- **Personalisation:** the AI slows down exactly where you slow down.
- **Structure:** our tracks give the AI a proven syllabus instead of a random walk.
- **A human backstop:** when the AI's explanation does not click, or you want a real engineer's opinion on your code, the [mentorship programme](/pages/mentorship/) is one email away.

## The honest limits

An AI teacher occasionally explains things confidently and wrongly. Cross-check against the chapter you are on, run every snippet it gives you, and treat disagreement between the AI and reality as a learning moment - reality wins.
