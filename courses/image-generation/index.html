Image Generation: From Pixels to Diffusion

Pankaj Doharey
July 29, 2026

Build image models from first principles, from convolutions and autoencoders to U-Nets, GANs and diffusion.

Continue learning

Build every idea behind modern image models in pure Python. No frameworks, no GPU - just the actual math, running in your browser.

Start with the convolution, the operation that lets networks see. Compress images with autoencoders, meet the U-Net that powers every serious image model, pit a generator against a critic in a GAN, and finish by building diffusion - the technology inside Stable Diffusion and DALL-E - from first principles.

Prerequisite: comfort with Neural Networks from Scratch in the sister course, Machine Learning: From Zero to LLMs.
